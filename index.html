The Orbit Journey — every touchpoint
01

Register

Every ticket becomes an Orbit identity — zero additional setup or downloads for the attendee. At signup, attendees set their intent: who they need to meet, what a "success" looks like. Orbit starts matching before anyone arrives.

Pre-event

Orbit sends personalised emails to every attendee in the lead-up to the event — so everyone arrives prepped to get the most from the technology, and from the event itself.

Arrive

Pick up your lanyard. Scan yourself into the network. Your first node lights up on the wall as you walk in. No additional steps.

Connect & remember

Every handshake becomes a scan, and every scan opens further opportunities. Add notes to your list of connections so no name — and no follow-up — is ever lost.

Experience

The room becomes a live interactive installation projected across the venue — growing by the minute, driving organic marketing and connection rates.

Expand

Orbit identifies the people you want to meet in your extended network — and exactly how to reach them.

After

Personal 'In Orbit' reports for attendees. Stand reports for exhibitors. The full map for organisers. The network keeps working.
